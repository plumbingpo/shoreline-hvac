How Tacoma Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
