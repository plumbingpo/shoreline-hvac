The Benefits of Professional AC Installation

The most costly installation mistakes are the ones you cannot see at startup. Skip a proper vacuum, and moisture left in the line set turns to acid that attacks the compressor over the next few summers. Guess at the refrigerant charge instead of weighing it in, and the system wastes efficiency every hour it runs. Pick the size by rule of thumb, and an oversized unit short-cycles toward an early failure while the house stays humid. Each of these problems shows up later as the very repair bills and high energy costs a new system was supposed to end.

Our experienced technicians do the unglamorous steps that make equipment last: nitrogen pressure testing before evacuation, a micron-gauge-verified vacuum, charge set by weight and confirmed against measured superheat and subcooling, condensate drainage with a proper trap and overflow protection, and airflow verified with static pressure readings rather than a hand held over a register. Refrigerant handling itself is federally regulated, and the recovery and evacuation equipment involved is not a hardware-store purchase. Maintenance Tips for AC Installation

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Follow these tips from our experienced technicians:

  • Avoid huge thermostat setbacks, which force long, hard runs during the hottest hours
  • Start a regular filter schedule on day one to protect the new coil from restricted airflow
  • Have a technician check the system every spring so charge and amp draws stay where they were set
  • Clear the condensate drain every spring before a clog can trip the float switch
  • Address small issues early; a soft capacitor caught in April never becomes a July breakdown
  • Hose off the outdoor unit occasionally and keep plants away from every side
  • Keep supply and return vents open to preserve the airflow balance set at commissioning
