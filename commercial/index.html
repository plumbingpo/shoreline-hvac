Kitchen Climate Control

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Shoreline HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your hours.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and customers notice the drafts and the heat right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Shoreline HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Preventive Care

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Shoreline HVAC program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Our commercial clients consistently report fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills after implementing a program.
